TagSpeech

End-to-end meeting transcription: who spoke what, and when

TagSpeech unifies multi-speaker ASR, speaker diarization, and timestamp prediction. Dual speech encoders and interleaved time anchors produce structured, speaker-attributed meeting transcripts.

  • End-to-end ASR
  • Diarization
  • Meeting transcription
  • Temporal grounding
  • Audio-language models
