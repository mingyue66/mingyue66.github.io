I am a PhD candidate in Computational Linguistics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Yan Tang. I expect to graduate in December 2026.

My research focuses on speech and multimodal AI. I work on foundation-model evaluation and post-training, drawing on speech representation learning, multi-speaker understanding, and human speech perception.

This fall, I am a research intern at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL), working on long-form audio understanding. Previously, I interned at Netflix, Tencent AI Lab, Amazon Prime Video, and ByteDance AI Lab.

I am seeking full-time Research Scientist roles and am available in Fall 2026. Please get in touch.
